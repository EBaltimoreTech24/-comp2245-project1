Fruit, vegetables, bread and herbs from small farms across the parish, sold by the people who grow them.

Why shop with us

Grown nearby

Everything on sale is grown or made within 50 km of the market, so it reaches you within a day of being picked.

In season

What you find changes through the year: mangoes in summer, sorrel at Christmas, and greens almost every week.

Meet the growers

Ask the farmers how they grow their food and how to cook it. Most stalls are run by the families who farm the land.

Visit the market

We set up in the community centre car park, with covered stalls when it rains.
